My dearest Alyssa

My love,

I don't think words will ever be enough to explain what you mean to me, but I still want to try.

I wasn't looking for someone when you came into my life. I wasn't waiting for someone either. You simply came into my life, unexpectedly, and somehow became someone incredibly important to me.

Sometimes I think about how beautiful it is that God allowed our paths to cross. I didn't plan it. I didn't expect it. You simply became part of my life, and I'm so grateful that you did.

Alyssa, I want you to know something. I chose you.

I chose you because you accepted me for who I am. You didn't ask me to become someone else before I could be loved by you.

You saw me as Daiske, with my flaws, imperfections, overthinking, weird sides, and all the little things that make me who I am.

And somehow, you still accepted me.

That means more to me than I could ever properly explain.

You made me feel like I could simply be myself around you.

And I want to give that same feeling back to you. You don't have to be perfect for me. You don't have to prove that you're enough.

You are enough simply by being Alyssa.

I love the little things about you. The way you talk, the way you laugh, your little habits, and all those tiny things you probably don't even notice about yourself.

Somehow, those little things became some of my favorite things in the world.

You were worth it all along.

And if I were given the chance to go back and choose all over again, knowing everything I know now...

I would still choose you.

Again. And again. And again.

I am incredibly grateful that God placed you into my life.

I love you, Lysa.

Always choosing you,
Daiske ♡
